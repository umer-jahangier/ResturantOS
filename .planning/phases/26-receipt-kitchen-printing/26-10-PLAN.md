<objective>
Give the printer registry a screen, and give columns-per-line the only honest source it can have —
a number a human counted on paper.

Research §7.5 is deliberate about this: it could not establish a trustworthy canonical column count
for any model, found a vendor datasheet stating a character size that is plainly wrong, and
concluded that column count is a function of model, configured print width, font and codepage, and
must be measured during branch onboarding by printing a ruler line. This plan builds the ruler.

It also builds the Test Print button, which is the single control that turns the whole ESC/POS tier
from a claim into an observation. A manager who presses it either gets a page or learns precisely
which of the three things went wrong.

Purpose: D-26-05's configuration requirement, and the scope fence's "printer configuration".
Output: a printing settings screen, a diagnostic page in the agent, and the ruler workflow.
</objective>

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-26-10-A | Repudiation | Test Print reporting success for a queued job | high | mitigate | Three outcomes, three messages; the collapse is explicitly asserted absent |
| T-26-10-B | Denial of Service | A silently incomplete configuration | high | mitigate | The unrouted-station notice is persistent and reappears on reload while the gap remains |
