<objective>
Ship the tier that needs no hardware: an itemised, correctly totalled bill on 80 mm paper, printed
from any browser through the operating system's own print pipeline.

This is the plan the demo depends on. Research §8 records that `window.print()` is the only
transport with roughly one hundred percent browser coverage, works on Safari and iPad where every
other option is permanently unavailable, and needs no install. It also records exactly what it
cannot do — no cash drawer, no cut, no choice of printer, and no way to know whether it printed.
That is why it is D-26-01's floor rather than its mechanism, and why this plan must not pretend
otherwise in its user interface.

Everything here is independently shippable. If plans 26-04 onwards never landed, a restaurant could
still take payment and hand the customer a bill, which is the whole reason the register puts an HTML
print bill in Tier 1 of the shortest path to demo-able.

Purpose: definition-of-done items 1, 5 and 6.
Output: a print-only receipt route, its stylesheet, the FBR placeholder regions, and the button on
the settlement screen.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| pos-service to the browser | A financial document crosses into a rendering surface a customer will read |
| the browser to the operating system print spooler | A rendered page leaves the product's control entirely |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-26-05-A | Tampering | Client-side money arithmetic on a receipt | critical | mitigate | The component renders server-supplied strings; a grep gate forbids formatting calls and a test asserts every currency token on the page exists in the document |
| T-26-05-B | Information Disclosure | Application shell printed on a customer's bill | low | mitigate | The print media block hides everything outside the receipt root; asserted in the component test and again in the browser under emulated print media |
| T-26-05-C | Repudiation | Claiming a print succeeded | medium | mitigate | No success toast; the browser provides no completion signal and the product does not invent one |
| T-26-05-D | Information Disclosure | Another tenant's order printed | critical | mitigate | The route reuses the order-view permission guard and the server-side tenant scoping from 26-03; no order data is fetched client-side by any other path |
| T-26-05-E | Tampering | A legally required FBR symbol rendered as a blank square | high | mitigate | An unavailable QR renders an explicit labelled state, never an empty square that reads as a printing fault |
| T-26-05-F | Denial of Service | A failed fetch rendering an empty bill | high | mitigate | `QueryBoundary` on the route; error precedes empty, and the empty bill case is asserted absent |
| T-26-05-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo installs | high | mitigate | This plan adds no package; the acceptance criteria assert no QR or barcode library entered the manifest |
</threat_model>

   the conservative content width chosen in task 1 is right or wasteful.

printer at all — including an A4 office printer — which is the point of this tier.
