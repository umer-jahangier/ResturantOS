<objective>
Give the kitchen a printed ticket, produced by the server that already knows which station cooks
what.

Research §9.3 decision 4 calls this the single biggest reliability win available in this phase, and
the reason is blunt: a kitchen ticket must print whether or not anybody is looking at a browser.
`POST /orders/{id}/send-to-kds` already exists, already knows the revision semantics, and already
publishes the event the kitchen display consumes. This plan hangs a print job off that same seam.

The ticket is enqueued here. Delivery to a physical printer with no browser open at all is plan
26-11's pull channel; until that lands, tickets drain through the browser bridge in 26-09. Either
way the job exists as a durable row the moment the order is fired, which is what makes the later
channel a delivery change rather than a redesign.

Purpose: the scope fence in 26-CONTEXT puts the kitchen ticket in scope, and D-26-05 requires the
routing to reach the station.
Output: a kitchen-ticket assembler, a dispatch service on the fire seam, and its tests.
</objective>
