| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-13-10-A | Information Disclosure | Temporary password in the provisioning response | high | mitigate | Returned once over TLS to an authenticated SuperAdmin; not logged, not persisted plaintext, not evented; the javadoc marks it as out-of-band material |
| T-13-10-B | Information Disclosure | Temporary password in the idempotency cache | high | mitigate | Explicitly acknowledged, its storage named in the SUMMARY, and bounded by the existing one-hour idempotency TTL |

| T-13-10-D | Denial of Service | A tenant provisioned into a locked-out state | critical | mitigate | The auth tenant row and the OWNER assignment are saga steps with compensation; the live script proves login end to end |
