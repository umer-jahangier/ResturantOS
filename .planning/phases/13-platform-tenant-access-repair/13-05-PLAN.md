## Resolved: where platform authentication lives (D-26, Claude's discretion)

**Decision: platform-admin-service**, as a new controller separate from `PlatformAdminController`.

Rationale: PLATFORM-07 is an explicit requirement that only platform-admin-service connects to
`platform_db`. Putting the login in auth-service would require either a second datasource in
auth-service or a cross-service credential read, and both violate that boundary. Splitting the
responsibility instead — platform-admin-service verifies the credential because it owns the table,
auth-service signs the token because it owns the RSA private key and the JWKS contract — preserves both
invariants and keeps a single signer for the whole platform.

**Consequence.** A platform login is two hops: the browser to platform-admin-service through the
gateway, then platform-admin-service to auth-service over the internal channel. The internal secret is
therefore load-bearing for platform authentication, which is why plan 13-01's minting endpoint asserts
a 403 on a missing secret and why threat T-13-05-B below is rated critical.

**Consequence.** The controller must be a new class. `PlatformAdminController` carries a class-level
authorization annotation requiring the SuperAdmin authority; a login method on that class could never
be called by an unauthenticated caller.
</decision_record>
