<objective>
Deliver tenant subscription and tier management (D-35), which CONTEXT.md places in scope, and close the
three platform-tier defects the audit rates most serious in section 2.2: the impersonation actor
inversion (D-34), the write-once tier, and the compiled-in quota.

The audit's phrasing on tier is the sharpest: the upgrade call-to-action header returned on every
disabled-feature response leads nowhere, because there is no way to change a tier. Every feature gate in
the product ends in a dead link.

Purpose: tier is the commercial control surface of the platform and it is currently write-once.
Output: tenant edit, tier change, subscription fields, retry, a corrected impersonation actor, and
per-tenant quota.
</objective>
