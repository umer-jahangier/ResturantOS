<objective>
Transform the three remaining expressive surfaces that exist today: the login screen, the SuperAdmin
console and the settings screens.

Login is the highest-stakes surface in this phase and the one where a cosmetic change does the most
damage. It is reachable without a session, it is where every user's day starts, and a broken submit
path there is not a visual regression — it is an outage. So the treatment here is deliberately
conservative in behaviour and ambitious only in surface, and the form's contract is asserted
unchanged before anything is restyled.

It is also where the unbounded-background temptation is strongest. A photographic or generative
backdrop behind a glass login card is the single most attractive thing an executor could add in this
phase and the one thing D-27-04 cannot survive, because contrast over an arbitrary image is not
measurable and an unmeasurable surface cannot carry a measured figure.

The settings screens bring the third thing: the appearance screen can move the brand hue at runtime,
and every glass token follows that hue. Phase 20 already discovered that a tenant hue near 262 walks
one chart series into another, and published a guard band as a result. The same question has to be
asked of glass, and answered by measurement rather than by the observation that glass is nearly
neutral.

Purpose: with 27-06 these complete definition-of-done item 2.
Output: three transformed surfaces, a brand-hue sweep over the glass guarantee, and evidence.
</objective>
