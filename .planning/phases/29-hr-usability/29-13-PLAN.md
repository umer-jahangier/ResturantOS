<objective>
Finish the two screens the phase has not yet touched.

The Attendance screen holds three separate ad-hoc forms in `useState` objects: a clock panel keyed off a
bare employee select, a leave request built from an employee id, a leave type id, two dates and a reason
with no validation of any kind, and a quarantine resolver where an operator types or picks an employee to
bind permanently to a device PIN. All three report failure with a fixed one-line toast, so an insufficient
leave balance, a date range entered backwards and a service outage look identical.

The Schedule screen holds a shift-creation strip whose days field is the comma-separated text box plan
29-11 replaces. Once settings owns shift definition, this screen's job is assignment, and keeping a second
creation path here is how the two drift.

Purpose: definition-of-done items 1, 2 and 3 across the last two HR screens, and the removal of the second
shift-creation path.
Output: three dialogs on the standard, two screens reduced to what they are actually for.
</objective>

<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| operator to biometric device mapping | Resolving a quarantined punch permanently binds a device PIN to a person |
| HR user to leave and attendance records | These feed late-arrival deductions and therefore payroll |
| branch operator to another branch's roster | The employee picker must not offer employees the server would refuse |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-29-13-A | Tampering | Misidentifying a quarantined punch, attributing attendance and pay to the wrong person | high | mitigate | The dialog shows the device evidence, states the permanence of the mapping, and requires deliberate confirmation; an already-mapped reference is refused with the existing holder named |
| T-29-13-B | Information Disclosure | An employee picker listing another branch's staff | high | mitigate | The picker is scoped to the active branch by its hook; hr-service enforces branch isolation through `hr.rego` regardless, and the cross-branch refusal is one of the asserted behaviours |
| T-29-13-C | Tampering | A leave request approved against the wrong record via a typed id | medium | mitigate | The typed-id control is removed; approval acts on the row it is rendered in |
| T-29-13-D | Information Disclosure | A failed quarantine load reading as an empty queue | medium | mitigate | Failure renders the retry notice, matching the roster treatment already on this screen |
| T-29-13-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo installs | high | mitigate | No new packages. If one becomes necessary, halt and require the package-legitimacy audit plus a blocking human checkpoint |
