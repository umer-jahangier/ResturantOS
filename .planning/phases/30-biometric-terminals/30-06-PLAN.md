<objective>
Give D-30-03 the thing it needs to be true: exactly three destinations for a line, and no way out
except through one of them.

The existing quarantine is the best-designed part of this integration. An unmapped reference is
parked with its raw line rather than dropped, resolving one writes a durable mapping so every future
punch for that reference auto-resolves, and re-pointing a reference at a second employee is refused.
Two things are missing from it, and one of them is a defect nobody has noticed.

The missing half: 30-05 made the parser report a rejection instead of returning nothing, but nothing
yet stores that rejection. A line that cannot be interpreted still ends nowhere, which is the same
unpaid hour by a different route.

The defect: **an unmapped punch is queued unconditionally, with no uniqueness key.** A device that
buffers through a network blip and replays its batch — the ordinary behaviour the protocol is built
around — produces a fresh queue entry for every unmapped punch on every replay. The existing replay
test uses a *mapped* reference, so it exercises the conflict clause on the punch table and never
touches this path. D-30-05 says a re-sent batch must not double-count; a queue an administrator has
to clear three times for one punch is double-counting in the surface that costs a person time.

Purpose: D-30-03's promise is only as strong as the queue's weakest property, and today that is its
uniqueness.
Output: a reasoned, deduplicated, resolvable and dismissible queue, and a test that a replay does not
grow it.
</objective>
