<objective>
Finish the conversion story (D-36-05): one resolver, computing the real ratio between the purchase
unit and the stock unit, returning nothing across incompatible families, asserted with a case a human
can check by hand — and stop the receipt from guessing when the resolver returns nothing.

The resolver exists. Phase 22 built it after proving the depletion half live: a recipe line of a
quarter kilogram against an ingredient stocked in kilograms depleted five hundred times too much and
posted a food cost of four thousand percent, with every event balanced and nothing failing. The same
phase fixed the mirror image on the receiving side, where a gram-priced pack into a kilogram-stocked
ingredient fell into a wrong-family branch and was received a thousandfold short.

What remains is the last silent path. When the resolver returns nothing — an unknown unit, or two
units in different families — the receipt still applies a factor of one and succeeds. Its author was
right at the time: throwing would have dead-lettered a batch after finance had already posted its
goods-received entry, turning a valuation error into a reconciliation gap. That justification no
longer holds. Finance now posts from the real stock lot, downstream of this code, so a refusal here
leaves no entry to reconcile against. And plan 31-04 refuses the same line at the API first, where a
human can fix it, so the refusal here is a last line rather than a first one.

Purpose: definition-of-done item 4, and the arithmetic every stock and cost number in this system
rests on.
Output: a hand-checkable assertion, a receipt that refuses instead of guessing, and a live proof.
</objective>
