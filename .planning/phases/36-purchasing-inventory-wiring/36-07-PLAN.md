<objective>
Make the seed able to create purchasing data, and make the credentials document tell the truth again
(definition-of-done item 6).

The seed already reports its own failure honestly rather than skipping quietly — that is why anyone
knew purchasing was empty. Two things kept it empty. The first is the permission gap plan 31-02
settled. The second is the seed's own: it builds each purchase-order line around an ingredient id it
generates, which inventory has never seen. Phase 22 traced exactly that id through a dead-lettered
receipt to a purchase order marked closed with no stock and no ledger entry, and identified the
generated identifier by its own version marker. After plan 31-04 that line is refused at the API,
which is correct and which means the seed must now do what a person does: pick an ingredient that
exists.

Purpose: a demo tenant with no procurement history makes every purchasing screen look broken, and a
credentials document carrying a fixed caveat trains readers to ignore the ones that are still true.
Output: a seed that completes the chain and asserts its results, and two corrected documents.
</objective>
