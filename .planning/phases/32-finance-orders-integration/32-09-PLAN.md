<objective>
The number a restaurant owner looks at first, every single day (D-32-02).

Gross sales, discounts, comps, tax, service charge, net, split by tender, against what each till
counted, with a cash variance shown as a variance and never silently absorbed. As of plans 32-03 and
32-06 the data behind every one of those exists and is correctly dated. This plan turns it into one
answer.

The design constraint that shapes everything here is D-32-05. The SuperAdmin console set the
precedent: metering had zero producers, so it rendered "Not metered" and named the reason rather than
a plausible zero. A takings screen has more ways to be confidently wrong than most — a till that never
closed, a comp category nothing populates, a day with orders but no tender rows — and every one of
them has a tempting zero. So the response type does not carry bare numbers. Every figure carries its
state: computed, or not computed with a reason. The renderer in 32-12 cannot then accidentally print a
zero, because there is no zero to print.

Purpose: an owner opens Finance and sees today's takings, reconciled, or is told exactly what is missing.
Output: one endpoint, every figure stated with its provenance, variances visible per till.
</objective>
