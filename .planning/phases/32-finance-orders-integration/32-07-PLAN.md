<objective>
Close 22b's D-8: zero of 115 item fact rows carry a cost, so there is no margin report, and "do the
reports agree with the ledger?" is unanswerable for cost of sales.

The ledger has the cost. `STOCK_DEPLETED` carries a per-ingredient breakdown and a total valued at
moving-average cost, and finance posts it. Reporting does not consume the event at all, and
`SalesFactWriter` writes an explicit null with a comment saying so, correctly — the order-closed
payload has never carried cost, and writing zero there would have claimed every plate was sold at
cost.

The interesting problem is grain. `DepletionService` walks each sold line's recipe, accumulates the
required quantity per ingredient across the whole order, and only then values it — so by the time
`DepletedLine` is built, which sold line drove which gram is gone. That aggregation exists for a
reason: FEFO lot selection must see the order's whole demand for an ingredient at once. This plan
does not undo it. It records the per-line consumption alongside the aggregate, and attributes cost
back by the quantity each line actually consumed. That is arithmetic on real quantities, not an
allocation rule.

22b's warning governs the verification design and is answered in plan 32-10: the GL-to-sub-ledger tie
held while cost was a thousand times wrong, because the over-depletion and its journal entry mirrored
each other faithfully. This plan's job is to land the number; 32-10's job is to check it in a way that
a mirrored error cannot pass.

Purpose: an owner cannot see margin because nothing stores cost against a sale.
Output: cost attributed per sold line, landed at the order's business date, tying exactly to the total.
</objective>
