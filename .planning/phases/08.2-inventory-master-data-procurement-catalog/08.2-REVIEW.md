## Critical Issues (3)

### CR-1 — Transfer ship/receive never validate the target branch against the caller's JWT branch claim
`services/inventory-service/.../web/TransferController.java:45-59` (from plan 08.2-17, a late scope deviation with no HTTP/OPA test).
`ship()`/`receive()` call only `authz.authorizeManage(tenantId, claims.branchId())` and never check `fromBranchId`/`toBranchId` (ship) or the transfer's stored `toBranchId` (receive) against the caller's branch — unlike every sibling endpoint (`StockLevelController`, `RecipeController.preview`, and `TransferController.pending` on the same controller). A Branch-A `inventory.item.manage` holder can move/receive stock at arbitrary branches in the tenant and perturb another branch's moving-average cost. **Cross-branch privilege escalation within a tenant.** Fix: mirror the sibling branch-ownership guard; add a `TransferAccessControlIT` at the HTTP/OPA level.

### CR-2 — Catalog-driven PO line silently prices at 0 when the vendor item has no current price
`services/purchasing-service/.../service/PurchaseOrderService.java:111-116` (plan 08.2-10).
A catalog line with no caller-supplied price and no recorded current price falls back to `unitPricePaisa = 0L` with no error and `priceOverridden=false`, so it looks like a normally-priced line. Because `submit()` derives required approval tiers from `totalPaisa`, a real order can under-report its value and require fewer approvals than warranted. Untested. Fix: throw a typed exception instead of defaulting to 0.

### CR-3 — PO line `qty` has no positivity constraint — negative qty can net down `totalPaisa` below approval thresholds
`services/purchasing-service/.../dto/CreatePurchaseOrderRequest.java:33` (plan 08.2-10 touched this file).
`Line.qty` is only `@NotNull` (no `@Positive`). A negative-qty line deflates the summed `totalPaisa`, and `submit()` derives `requiredTiers` from that total — an approval-tier bypass vector. Fix: add `@Positive` to `Line.qty`.
