<objective>
The screen an owner opens first, every day (D-37-02).

Today `/app/finance` redirects to Accounts — a chart of accounts, which is the least useful thing an
owner could be shown first. This plan makes the landing screen the day's takings: gross sales,
discounts, comps, tax, service charge, net, split by tender, set against what each till counted, with
every variance visible as a variance.

Plan 32-09 made this possible and made it honest, by returning every figure with its state rather than
as a bare number. This plan's job is to not undo that. The whole value of a two-state figure is lost
the moment a component reads the amount and renders zero when it is absent, so the component switches
on the state and there is a dedicated element for the not-computed case that renders the reason where
the number would have been. That element is reusable, and plan 32-13 explains it to the owner.

The precedent is the SuperAdmin console's usage panel: metering had no producers, so it said "Not
metered" and named the reason rather than drawing a zero-percent bar. A zero on a takings screen is a
stronger claim than a zero on a usage screen, and this screen is the one people make decisions on.

Purpose: an owner opens Finance and sees today's takings, reconciled, or is told what is missing.
Output: the finance landing screen, with variances per till and unknowns stated as unknowns.
</objective>
