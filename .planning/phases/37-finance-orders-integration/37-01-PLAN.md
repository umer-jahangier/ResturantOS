<objective>
Settle the money-display disagreement before eleven new finance surfaces inherit it. Today three
renderers exist and two of them disagree: `MoneyUtils.formatPkr` calls `setMaximumFractionDigits(0)`
and renders 123456 paisa as a whole-rupee figure, while the frontend's `toMoney` and `MoneyDisplay`
render two places. Phase 26 already discovered this and wrote a fourth, `ReceiptMoneyFormatter`,
whose javadoc explains precisely why the backend one is wrong.

This plan declares the two-decimal rendering authoritative, makes `MoneyUtils.formatPkr` delegate to
the existing authority instead of contradicting it, collapses the frontend's two entry points onto
one helper, and pins the whole thing with a golden-vector file that BOTH stacks' tests read. After
this, a paisa integer has exactly one display string in this product, and a change on either side is
a red test on the other.

Every screen in plans 32-09 through 32-13 renders money. They all cite this plan.

Purpose: an owner comparing a screen to a printed bill to the ledger must not find three numbers.
Output: one display rule, one shared vector file, two tests that cannot drift apart.
</objective>
