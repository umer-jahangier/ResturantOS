<objective>
Close 22b's D-7: 9,492,000 paisa of revenue that the general ledger dates 2026-08-06 and the sales
report dates 2026-08-07, across 73 orders.

The amounts already tie to the paisa — 22b proved that on all three days tested — so the revenue
wiring is sound and only the day bucket is wrong. pos-service resolves `businessDate` once, checks
the accounting period against it, and puts it on the ORDER_CLOSED payload. finance dates the journal
entry from that field, which is why finance and pos never disagree. reporting ignores the field and
re-derives the date from `closedAt` against the branch timezone; for a UTC+5 branch the two formulas
diverge for anything closed in the early-morning window.

This is the defect that makes daily takings wrong at exactly the moment an owner checks them, so it
lands before the takings screen is built rather than after. Plans 32-06, 32-07 and 32-09 all bucket
by business date and all depend on this being right.

Purpose: one trading day per sale, decided once, honoured everywhere.
Output: the consumer reads the field, existing rows are realigned with evidence, and a reconciliation
script proves ledger and report agree across a day boundary.
</objective>
