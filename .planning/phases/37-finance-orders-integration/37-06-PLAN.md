<objective>
Daily takings is "split by tender, against what each till counted" (D-37-02). Today nothing stores
tenders at all.

`OrderClosedPayload` has carried a `payments` list since phase 7 — method, amount applied, amount
tendered, change given, reference number — and reporting's mirror record keeps three of those five
fields and writes none of them anywhere. `sales_order_facts` has a total and no breakdown. So the
question "how much of today came in as cash?" has no data behind it, and the only honest answer a
takings screen could give today is that it does not know.

This plan lands the missing fact table so the screen in 32-09 and 32-12 can answer it with real
numbers rather than a plausible split.

One distinction carries the whole plan. `amountPaisa` is what was applied to the bill;
`tenderedPaisa` is what the customer handed over; `changePaisa` is what came back. Takings is the
first. Recording the second would overstate every cash day by the change given, and it would do so
invisibly, because the total would still look like money.

Purpose: a tender split cannot be reported from a table that has no tenders in it.
Output: a payment fact table, its writer, and a reconciliation that ties tenders to order totals.
</objective>

  <behavior>
    - A single-tender cash order produces one row whose applied amount equals the order total and whose tendered amount is at least that.
    - A split-tender order across cash and card produces two rows whose applied amounts sum to the order total.
    - A card row carries no change and no till session, and the absence is recorded as absence rather than as zero.
    - Every payment row carries the same business date as its order row, taken from the producer's field.
    - An order whose payments list is absent or empty is rejected to the dead-letter queue naming the order id, because a closed order with no tender is a producer defect and must be loud.

    - Redelivery of the same event produces no additional visible rows once merged.
