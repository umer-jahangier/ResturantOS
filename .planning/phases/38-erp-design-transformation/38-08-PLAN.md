# 38-08 — Customers, staff and finance

## Goal

Bring the nine finance tables onto the grid without weakening the one screen in this product that
already gets it right.

## Why

**Finance owns the largest concentration of hand-rolled tables in the codebase** — nine of the
thirty-three: `AccountTable`, `ApAgingTable`, `ArAgingTable`, `GeneralLedger`,
`JournalEntryTable`, `TenderSplit`, `TillVariancePanel`, `TransactionLedgerLinks`,
`TransactionRegister`. `components/finance/` is also the largest component directory in the
product (30 files) and carries raw palette literals in four of them
(`TillVariancePanel` 8, `TransactionRegister` 3, `PeriodCloseModal` 3, `PeriodStatusChip` 2).

**And `/app/finance/takings` is the best screen in the product.** The audit says so plainly. It
renders `Not known` with a stated reason (*"Comps are not recorded separately from discounts"*),
it refuses to sum variances across tills (*"two drawers out by opposite amounts is two problems,
and one total of zero would hide both"*), and it prints *"Cash variance for the day: not known —
This is NOT a zero variance."* That is phase 14b and phase 37 working exactly as designed, and it
is the standard the other eight tables are held to — not the thing this plan tidies away.

**HR could not be audited.** `hr-service` has no process on `:8088`, is absent from Eureka, and
returns `503`. `/app/hr/employees` and `/app/hr/attendance` could not be captured clean in four
attempts, in either theme. Their defects below are source-derived only.

## Tasks

1. **Migrate the nine finance tables to `DataGrid`** — sticky headers, one row height, pagination
   or virtualisation on the general ledger, shared `StatusBadge`, card fallback below `md`.

2. **Money through `MoneyDisplay` only.** BIGINT paisa, one formatter, so screen, printed bill and
   ledger agree to the paisa. Negatives keep **parentheses** so the sign survives greyscale and
   dichromacy. **No second formatting path** — not in a tooltip, not in a CSV export, not in a
   print template.

3. **One money typeface.** `/app/finance/takings` renders money in **monospace**; UI-SPEC §3.11
   reserves Geist Mono for identifiers and makes every number tabular in `@layer base`. Money
   converges to tabular Geist Sans. This is the only change made to that screen.

4. **Preserve every honesty affordance.** `Not known` with a reason, un-summed variances, the
   explicit "this is NOT a zero variance" — asserted by test, so a later refactor cannot quietly
   turn an unknown into a zero. Phase 14b exists because eleven screens told an owner his business
   had no vendors when the service was down.

5. **CRM and HR lists to `DataGrid`**, `PageHeader` on each, `StatusBadge` and `ConfirmDialog`
   from 38-03.

6. **`/app/hr/attendance`** — 6 unlabelled inputs, 3 unnamed controls, `h1Count = 0`.
   **Re-audit in a browser against a running `hr-service` before executing.**

7. Token convergence: G3 baselines for the four finance files decrease to 0.

## Verification

| Check | Kind | Passes when |
|---|---|---|
| Grid conformance | e2e | sticky header, one row height, pagination on all nine finance tables |
| **One money path** | unit | `grep`: no currency formatting outside `MoneyDisplay`; a second `toFixed(2)` on a paisa value fails |
| Negative money | unit | renders with parentheses; asserted in greyscale-equivalent terms (no colour dependency) |
| **Honesty affordances survive** | unit | `Not known` renders with its reason; variances are not summed; the "NOT a zero variance" string is present |
| Money typeface | e2e | computed `font-family` on a money node is the sans stack; `font-variant-numeric` is tabular |
| Labels | e2e | 0 unlabelled inputs on every reachable route |
| `h1` | e2e | exactly 1 per route |
| Literals | unit | G3 baselines → 0 for the four finance files |

### Negative controls — observe each failing

1. Replace one `MoneyDisplay` with `${(paisa/100).toFixed(2)}` → one-money-path check red.
2. Sum the per-till variances into a single total → honesty check red. **This is the most
   important control in the plan**: the screen's whole argument is that the total would hide two
   opposite problems.
3. Change a `Not known` to `Rs 0.00` → honesty check red.
4. Drop the parentheses on a negative → negative-money check red.
5. Remove a label from an attendance input → label check red.

> **Do not anchor any gate on an HR route while `hr-service` is down.** It will *skip*, silently,
> and report green — vacuous-gate pattern #4 from `SURFACE-MOTION-SPEC.md` §7. If the service is
> down at execution time, report the HR gates as **not run**.
