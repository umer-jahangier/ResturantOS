<objective>
Make approval limits tenant-managed from inside the product (D-31-03). An owner sets them per user,
or for every current holder of a role at a branch in one action, and the number they set is the one
the policy engine reads.

The limit column has existed since the branch-role table was created and the assignment endpoint has
always accepted it. What has never existed is any way to set it without a script: the create-user
request has no field for it, so every account starts with the column null, and three policies —
vendor, finance and point-of-sale — compare a resource amount against that attribute and deny when it
is absent. That is why nobody could approve a purchase order, and why the seed had to re-assign every
persona's role immediately after creating it purely to write a number. The seed's workaround is not
the defect; the absence of the field in the product is.

Purpose: plan 31-08 signs off on an owner completing the chain in the browser, and approval is a step
in that chain.
Output: an explicit limit decision at assignment time, a visible current limit, a bulk apply, and an
honest statement of when a change takes effect.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| owner to user-service | A spending authority is granted to another account |
| auth-service to the token | The limit becomes a signed claim the policy engine trusts |
| policy engine to the resource amount | An approval decision compares a caller attribute against money |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-31-03-A | Elevation of Privilege | Self-granted spending authority | critical | mitigate | The assignment endpoint is gated on the role-management authority split out in 13-02 and enforces the caller's own role ceiling server-side; the form adds no client-side bypass and sends no user id it did not receive |
| T-31-03-B | Elevation of Privilege | A limit surviving a role change | high | mitigate | The limit is written from the request on every assignment, and the dialog requires an explicit decision whenever the selected role holds an amount-gated permission |
