  <behavior>
TaxSlab record: (long minPaisa, Long maxPaisa /*null = open top*/, long baseTaxPaisa, double ratePct).
computeAnnualTax(long annualTaxableIncomePaisa, List<TaxSlab> slabs) -> long: find the bracket where income >= minPaisa AND (maxPaisa==null OR income < maxPaisa); tax = baseTaxPaisa + round(excess-over-min * ratePct/100). Add computeSurcharge(long tax, long income, long surchargeThresholdPaisa, double surchargeRatePct) applied to TAX (not income) only when income > threshold.
Cases (using the FY2025-26 seed slabs, income in PKR->paisa; ×100000 for lakh):
  - 600,000 PKR (60000000 paisa) -> 0 tax (top of 0% band, exclusive upper).
  - 1,200,000 -> 6,000 PKR tax (1% of 600k).
  - 2,200,000 -> 116,000 PKR (6,000 + 11% of 1,000,000).
  - 5,000,000 -> 616,000 + 35% of 900,000 = 931,000 PKR.
  - Surcharge: income 12,000,000 with 9% surcharge over 10,000,000 threshold -> tax*1.09.
  - income below first min or empty slabs -> defensive (0 or IllegalState per RESEARCH sketch); test the chosen contract.
  </behavior>
  <implementation>Implement exactly the RESEARCH SlabTaxCalculator sketch after tests are red. No if/else rate ladder — walk the slab list.</implementation>
</feature>
